EpiChat is a conversational agent that turns a plain-language question into an agent-based simulation in Starsim, grounds it in published estimates and country data, and explains what it found, so decision-makers can explore scenarios themselves, without waiting for a modeler.

[01 / context]
The bottleneck

The bottleneck in computational epidemiology is expertise, not compute.

During COVID-19, demand for modeling vastly outpaced supply. WHO and Africa CDC flagged this as a critical pandemic-preparedness gap — especially in LMICs with limited domestic modeling capacity.

Policy officers, surveillance teams, and health analysts need fast, defensible answers — but the modelers who can give them are a narrow bottleneck.

[02 / architecture]
Six stages

The model handles language. Validated tools handle the simulation.

A conversation moves through six stages, shown under the chat as a step strip. At each one the agent calls a tool; the tool, not the model, writes the configuration, applies the data and runs the simulation, and its result appears as a card you can inspect.

A question, step by step.

▸ YOUR QUESTION
"Model a measles outbreak in Kenya at current vaccination coverage."
▼ CONFIGURATION · WITH SOURCES
disease
measles · SEIR database
R₀
15 → β calibrated database, range 12–18
periods
latent 12 d · infectious 8 d database
fatality
1.5% of infections database
population
Kenya's ages, births and deaths UN WPP 2024
network
age-structured Prem 2021 matrix for Kenya
immune
88% at the start WHO/UNICEF first dose, 2024
seeding
0.05% of agents default
run
10,000 agents · 365 days · 5 runs
── understand · configure · ground in data · run · interpret · report ──
▸ REPLY

"In the median run of five the outbreak peaks around day 111 and 9.9% of the population is infected within the year. A campaign vaccinating 70% between days 48 and 78 brings that to 1.45%, with the peak on day 78."

▸ TRY NEXT
Vaccinate 70% in a campaign from day 48 to day 78 and rerun Cut contacts by 30% from day 48 for eight weeks and rerun Add masks (20% less transmission) from day 48 for 90 days and rerun
